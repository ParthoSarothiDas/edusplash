# 📍 Prepositions – Position Words

**Prepositions** are **position words**. 🌟 They tell us **where** or **when** someone or something is.

They help us know the **place** or **position** of a person, animal, or thing.

## 🧸 Common Prepositions

* 📦 **in**
* 🪑 **on**
* ⬇️ **under**
* 🚪 **behind**
* 🚶 **in front of**
* 📍 **between**
* 📫 **near**
* ✈️ **above**
* ⬇️ **below**
* ➡️ **beside**

## 🌈 Examples

* The cat is **on** the table. 🐱
* The ball is **under** the chair. ⚽
* The fish is **in** the pond. 🐟
* The boy is standing **behind** the tree. 🌳
* The girl is **in front of** the school. 🏫
* The rabbit is **between** the boxes. 🐰
* The bus stopped **near** the market. 🚌
* The bird is flying **above** the house. 🐦
* The shoes are **below** the bed. 👟
* The dog is sitting **beside** its owner. 🐶

## ⭐ Remember

**Prepositions** tell us **where** or **when** something happens.

### Easy to Remember

📦 **in** → inside
🪑 **on** → on top of
⬇️ **under** → below
🚪 **behind** → at the back of
🚶 **in front of** → before something
📍 **between** → in the middle of two things
📫 **near** → close to

🎉 **Prepositions help us find the place of people and things!**
