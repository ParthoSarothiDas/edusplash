# Pronouns

A **Pronoun** is a word that is used **instead of a Naming Word (noun)**. It helps us avoid repeating the same name again and again.

### Common Pronouns

* **I**
* **You**
* **He**
* **She**
* **It**
* **We**
* **They**

### Examples

* **Rina** is my friend. **She** is kind.
* **Rahim** is playing. **He** is happy.
* The **cat** is sleeping. **It** is tired.
* **Tom and Jerry** are friends. **They** play together.
* **Mina and I** are classmates. **We** study together.

### Remember

A **Pronoun** takes the place of a **Naming Word**.

**Example:**

* Rahim → **He**
* Rina → **She**
* Dog → **It**
* Rahim and Karim → **They**
* Mina and I → **We**
