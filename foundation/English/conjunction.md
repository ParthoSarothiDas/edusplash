# 🔗 Conjunctions – Joining Words

**Conjunctions** are **joining words**. 🌟 They join **words**, **groups of words**, or **sentences** together.

They make our sentences smooth and complete.

## 🌈 Common Conjunctions

* 💙 **and**
* ❤️ **but**
* 💛 **or**
* 💚 **because**
* 💜 **so**

## ⭐ How We Use Them

### 💙 **and** – joins two ideas

* Tom **and** Jerry are friends.
* I like apples **and** bananas.

### ❤️ **but** – shows a difference

* Rina is tired, **but** she is happy.
* The book is old **but** interesting.

### 💛 **or** – gives a choice

* Would you like tea **or** coffee?
* Do you want a pencil **or** a pen?

### 💚 **because** – tells the reason

* We stayed home **because** it was raining.
* She smiled **because** she was happy.

### 💜 **so** – tells the result

* It was raining, **so** we took an umbrella.
* He studied hard, **so** he passed the test.

## 🌟 Remember

🔹 **and** → joins things together
🔹 **but** → shows a difference
🔹 **or** → gives a choice
🔹 **because** → tells the reason
🔹 **so** → tells the result

🎉 **Conjunctions are little joining words that connect ideas and make sentences stronger!**
