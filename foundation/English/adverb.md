# ✨ Adverbs – Action Helper Words

**Adverbs** are **action helper words**. 🌟 They tell us **how**, **when**, or **where** an action happens.

They give us more information about an **Action Word (verb)**.

## 🌈 Adverbs Tell Us...

### 🚀 How?

* The rabbit runs **fast**.
* The turtle walks **slowly**.
* She sings **beautifully**.

### ⏰ When?

* We will play **today**.
* He came **early**.
* They will visit **tomorrow**.

### 📍 Where?

* The bird flew **away**.
* Come **here**.
* The children are playing **outside**.

## ⭐ Examples

* The baby slept **quietly**. 😴
* Rina answered **correctly**. 📚
* The dog barked **loudly**. 🐶
* We reached school **early**. 🏫
* The kite flew **high**. 🪁

## 🌟 Remember

An **Adverb** tells us more about an **Action Word**.

It tells us:

* 💨 **How?** → fast, slowly, happily
* ⏰ **When?** → today, tomorrow, early
* 📍 **Where?** → here, there, outside

🎉 **Adverbs make action words more interesting and help us imagine the action better!**
