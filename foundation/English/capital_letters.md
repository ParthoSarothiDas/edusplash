# 🔠 Capital Letters – Big Letters

**Capital Letters** are **big letters**. 🌟 We use them to make our writing **correct, neat, and easy to read**.

## 🌈 When Do We Use Capital Letters?

### 🟢 1. At the beginning of a sentence

* **The** sun is shining.
* **We** are going to school.

### 👦 2. For people's names

* **Rahim**
* **Rina**
* **Hasan**

### 🌍 3. For places

* **Dhaka**
* **Bangladesh**
* **Cox's Bazar**

### 📅 4. For days of the week

* **Monday**
* **Friday**
* **Sunday**

### 🗓️ 5. For months of the year

* **January**
* **May**
* **December**

### 📚 6. For the word **I**

* **I** like reading.
* **I** am happy.

## ⭐ Examples

* **Rahim** lives in **Dhaka**.
* **We** visited **Bangladesh** last year.
* My birthday is in **July**.
* **Monday** is the first school day of the week.
* **I** have a new bicycle.

## 🌟 Remember

Use **Capital Letters**:

* ✨ At the **beginning of every sentence**
* 👤 For **people's names**
* 🌍 For **places and countries**
* 📅 For **days of the week**
* 🗓️ For **months of the year**
* 🧒 For the word **I**

🎉 **Capital Letters help our writing look clear, correct, and beautiful!**
