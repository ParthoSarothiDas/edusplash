# 🌈 Be Verbs – Magic Helping Words

**Be Verbs** are **special helping words**. ✨ They tell us **who someone is**, **what something is**, or **how someone feels**.

The three most important Be Verbs are:

* 💙 **am**
* 💚 **is**
* 🧡 **are**

## 🌟 How to Use Them

* 👧 **I** → **am**
* 👦 **He** → **is**
* 👧 **She** → **is**
* 🐶 **It** → **is**
* 😊 **You** → **are**
* 👨‍👩‍👧 **We** → **are**
* 👧👦 **They** → **are**

## 🎈 Examples

* I **am** happy. 😊
* She **is** my best friend. 💖
* He **is** strong. 💪
* It **is** a cute puppy. 🐶
* We **are** ready to learn. 📚
* They **are** playing in the park. ⚽
* You **are** amazing! ⭐

## 🌟 Remember the Magic Rule

🟦 **I → am**
🟩 **He / She / It → is**
🟨 **You / We / They → are**

🎉 **Be Verbs help every sentence shine!** Just choose the correct magic word—**am**, **is**, or **are**.
