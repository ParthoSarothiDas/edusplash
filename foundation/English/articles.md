# 🍎 Articles – Tiny Helper Words

**Articles** are **small helper words**. 🌟 They come **before a Naming Word (noun)** and help us talk about people, animals, places, and things.

There are **three articles**:

* 🟢 **a**
* 🟡 **an**
* 🔵 **the**

## 🍏 Use **a**

Use **a** before a word that begins with a **consonant sound**.

### Examples

* a 🐱 cat
* a 🏀 ball
* a 🚗 car
* a 🌳 tree

## 🍎 Use **an**

Use **an** before a word that begins with a **vowel sound**.

The vowels are: **A, E, I, O, U**

### Examples

* an 🍎 apple
* an 🐘 elephant
* an ☂️ umbrella
* an 🥚 egg

## 👑 Use **the**

Use **the** when we are talking about **a special or known person, place, or thing**.

### Examples

* the 🌞 sun
* the 🌙 moon
* the 📖 book on the table
* the 🐅 tiger in the zoo

## 🌈 Remember

🟢 **a** → before words with a **consonant sound**
**a** cat, **a** dog, **a** pencil

🟡 **an** → before words with a **vowel sound**
**an** apple, **an** orange, **an** elephant

🔵 **the** → for **a special or known** person, place, or thing
**the** sun, **the** moon, **the** Taj Mahal

## ⭐ Fun Tip

Think of **Articles** as **little helpers** that stand in front of Naming Words and make every sentence sound just right! 🎉
