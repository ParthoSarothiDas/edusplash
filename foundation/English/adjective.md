# Describing Words (Adjectives)

**Describing Words** tell us more about a **Naming Word (noun)**. They describe a person, animal, place, or thing.

Describing words can tell us:

* **Size:** big, small, tall, short
* **Color:** red, blue, green
* **Shape:** round, square
* **Number:** one, two, many
* **Feeling or Quality:** happy, kind, clever
* **Condition:** clean, dirty, old, new

### Examples

* The **big** elephant is walking.
* She has a **red** bag.
* It is a **cold** day.
* The **happy** boy is smiling.
* We saw a **beautiful** flower.

### Remember

A **Describing Word** tells us **what kind**, **which one**, or **how many** about a naming word.

**Example:**

* **small** cat
* **blue** sky
* **three** apples
* **kind** teacher
