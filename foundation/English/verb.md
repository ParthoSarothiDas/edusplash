# Action Words (Verbs)

**Action Words** tell us **what a person, animal, or thing does**. They show an action or activity.

### Examples

* run
* jump
* eat
* read
* write
* sing
* play
* swim

### Look at these sentences:

* The boy **runs** fast.
* The bird **flies** in the sky.
* We **read** books every day.
* The cat **sleeps** on the mat.

The highlighted words are **Action Words** because they tell us what someone or something is doing.

### Remember

Action Words tell us what someone or something **does**.

Examples:

* 🏃 **run**
* 📖 **read**
* ✍️ **write**
* 🎵 **sing**
* ⚽ **play**
* 🍎 **eat**
* 🏊 **swim**
* 😴 **sleep**
