# 🎉 Interjections – Feeling Words

**Interjections** are **feeling words**. 🌟 They show **strong emotions or sudden feelings**.

We use them when we are happy, surprised, hurt, excited, or worried.

## 🌈 Common Interjections

* 😃 **Wow!**
* 🎉 **Hooray!**
* 🤕 **Ouch!**
* 😮 **Oops!**
* 😋 **Yummy!**
* ❤️ **Aww!**
* ⚠️ **Watch out!**
* 😟 **Oh no!**
* 🎊 **Yay!**
* 👏 **Bravo!**

## ⭐ Examples

* **Wow!** That rainbow is beautiful. 🌈
* **Hooray!** We won the match. 🏆
* **Ouch!** I hurt my finger. 🤕
* **Oops!** I dropped my pencil. ✏️
* **Yummy!** This cake is delicious. 🍰
* **Aww!** The puppy is so cute. 🐶
* **Watch out!** The ball is coming! ⚽
* **Oh no!** I forgot my homework. 📚
* **Yay!** We are going on a picnic! 🧺
* **Bravo!** You did a great job! 👏

## 🌟 Remember

An **Interjection** shows a **feeling or emotion**.

It can show:

* 😊 Happiness
* 😮 Surprise
* 🤕 Pain
* 😟 Sadness
* 🎉 Excitement
* ⚠️ Warning
* 👏 Praise

🎈 **Most interjections are followed by an exclamation mark (!) because they express strong feelings.**

🎉 **Interjections make our sentences lively, fun, and full of emotion!**
