# Naming Words (Nouns)

A **Naming Word** is the name of a **person, place, animal, or thing**. Naming words help us know who or what we are talking about.

### Examples

* **Person:** boy, girl, teacher, doctor
* **Place:** school, park, village, Dhaka
* **Animal:** cat, dog, elephant, bird
* **Thing:** book, ball, table, pencil

### Look at these sentences:

* The **boy** is reading a **book**.
* A **cat** is sleeping on the **chair**.
* We go to **school** every day.
* **Rina** has a **pencil**.

The highlighted words are **Naming Words** because they name a person, place, animal, or thing.

### Remember

A Naming Word can name:

* 👦 A **Person** – mother, Rahim, teacher
* 📍 A **Place** – school, park, Bangladesh
* 🐶 An **Animal** – dog, lion, fish
* 📚 A **Thing** – pen, ball, chair
