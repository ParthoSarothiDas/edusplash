# 🌤️ Weather and Seasons

Weather tells us what the air is like **today**.

Some days are sunny.
Some days are rainy.
Some days are cloudy.
Some days are windy.

---

# ☀️ Sunny Weather

A sunny day is bright.

☀️ The Sun shines.
😎 We can wear sunglasses or a hat.
💧 Drink plenty of water on hot days.

---

# 🌧️ Rainy Weather

A rainy day is wet.

🌧️ Rain falls from the clouds.
☂️ Use an umbrella or wear a raincoat.
👢 Rain boots help keep your feet dry.

---

# ☁️ Cloudy Weather

A cloudy day has many clouds.

☁️ The Sun may be hidden.
🌥️ The sky looks gray or white.

---

# 💨 Windy Weather

Wind is moving air.

🍃 Trees and leaves move in the wind.
🪁 Windy days are great for flying a kite.

---

# 👕 What to Wear

Choose clothes that match the weather.

| Weather | What to Wear or Use |
|---------|----------------------|
| ☀️ Sunny | Hat, Sunglasses |
| 🌧️ Rainy | Umbrella, Raincoat |
| 🥶 Cold | Jacket, Sweater |
| 💨 Windy | Light Jacket |

---

# 🌸 The Four Seasons

There are **four seasons**.

## 🌸 Spring

🌼 Flowers bloom.
🌱 Plants begin to grow.
😊 The weather is warm and pleasant.

---

## ☀️ Summer

🥵 Summer is usually hot.
👕 Wear light clothes.
💧 Drink plenty of water.
🏊 People like to swim.

---

## 🍂 Autumn (Fall)

🍁 Leaves change color and fall from trees.
🌬️ The weather becomes cooler.

---

## ❄️ Winter

🥶 Winter is the coldest season.
🧥 Wear warm clothes like jackets and sweaters.
☕ We like to keep warm.

---

# 🎉 Fun Activities

☀️ Sunny → Have a picnic or play outside.

🌧️ Rainy → Use an umbrella and splash in puddles safely.

💨 Windy → Fly a kite.

❄️ Winter → Wear warm clothes and play outside if it is safe.

---
