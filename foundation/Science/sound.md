# 🔊 Sound

Sound is all around us. We hear sounds every day. Sound helps us know what is happening around us.

## 🎵 Things That Make Sound

Many things can make sounds.

Examples:

* 🔔 Bell
* 🥁 Drum
* 🎸 Guitar
* 👏 Clapping hands
* 👦 People talking
* 🐶 Dog barking
* 🐱 Cat meowing
* 🐦 Bird chirping

Some things, like a **book** or a **stone**, do not make sound by themselves.

---

# 🔊 Loud and Soft Sounds

Some sounds are **loud**.

Examples:

* ⛈️ Thunder
* 🎆 Fireworks
* 🥁 Drum
* 📯 Car horn

Some sounds are **soft**.

Examples:

* 🤫 Whisper
* 🍃 Leaves rustling
* 🕒 Clock ticking
* 🎵 Soft music

# 🏡 Sounds Around Us

We hear many sounds every day.

* 🔔 School bell tells us it is time for class.
* 📯 A car horn warns people.
* 👏 Clapping shows happiness.
* 👩‍🏫 Your teacher's voice helps you learn.
* ⏰ An alarm clock helps you wake up.

---

# 👂 Our Ears

We hear sounds with our **ears**.

Our ears help us:

* 👂 Listen to our teacher.
* 🎵 Enjoy music.
* 😊 Talk with our friends.
* 🚗 Hear car horns and stay safe.

Take care of your ears.

* 😊 Listen at a safe volume.
* 🚫 Do not stay near very loud sounds for a long time.
* ✋ Cover your ears if a sound is too loud.
