# 🩺 Safety and Health

Good habits help us stay healthy, strong, and safe.

---

# 🥗 Healthy Food

Healthy food helps our body grow.

Examples of healthy foods:

🍎 Fruits  
🥕 Vegetables  
🥚 Eggs  
🥛 Milk  

Remember:
- Drink plenty of water.
- Eat fruits and vegetables every day.
- Eat less candy, chips, and junk food.

---

# 🏃 Exercise and Sleep

Exercise keeps our body strong and healthy.

Examples:
- 🚶 Walking
- 🏃 Running
- ⚽ Playing outside

Sleep helps our body rest and grow.

Remember:
- Exercise every day.
- Get enough sleep at night.

---

# 🧼 Keep Clean

Keeping clean helps remove germs.

Remember:
- 👐 Wash your hands with soap and water.
- Wash hands before eating.
- Wash hands after using the toilet.
- 🪥 Brush your teeth twice a day.
- Wear clean clothes.

---

# 🏠 Keep Places Clean

Clean places help us stay healthy.

Remember:
- 🗑️ Put rubbish in a dustbin.
- Keep your home and classroom clean.
- Cover food to keep it safe.
- Do not throw trash on the ground.

---

# 🤕 Small Injuries

Small cuts need care.

If you get a small cut:
- Wash it with clean water.
- Tell an adult.
- Use a bandage.

Always ask an adult for help when you are hurt.

---

# 🚸 Safety Rules

Follow safety rules every day.

Remember:
- Do not touch hot objects.
- Do not play with dangerous things.
- Wear a helmet when riding a bicycle.
- Look both ways before crossing the road.

---

# 🌟 Remember

🥗 Eat healthy food.  
💧 Drink water.  
🏃 Exercise every day.  
😴 Sleep well.  
🧼 Keep your body clean.  
🗑️ Use a dustbin.  
🤕 Tell an adult if you get hurt.  
🚸 Follow safety rules.

Healthy habits keep us safe and strong!
