# 🌍 Environment

Our environment is everything around us. It includes air, water, land, plants, animals, and people. We should take care of our Earth and keep it clean.

## ♻️ Reduce, Reuse, Recycle

### Reduce

Reduce means **use less**.

Examples:

* Turn off the tap when you do not need water.
* Switch off lights and fans when you leave a room.
* Use only the paper you need.
* Avoid wasting food and water.

### Reuse

Reuse means **use something again**.

Examples:

* Use a cloth bag many times.
* Use an old box to store toys.
* Use a glass jar to keep things.
* Write on both sides of paper.

### Recycle

Recycle means **make new things from old materials**.

Things that can be recycled:

* Paper
* Plastic
* Glass
* Metal

Put recyclable items in the correct recycling bin.

## 🗑️ Keeping Our Environment Clean

Pollution makes our environment dirty.

Ways to keep places clean:

* Throw rubbish in a dustbin.
* Do not throw trash on the ground.
* Keep rivers and parks clean.
* Do not burn plastic.
* Keep our classroom and home clean.

## 🌳 Protecting Nature

Plants and animals are important parts of our environment.

We can protect nature by:

* Planting trees.
* Saving water.
* Protecting animals.
* Keeping forests and rivers clean.
* Using less plastic.

## 💧 Save Water and Electricity

Save water:

* Turn off taps after use.
* Do not waste water while playing.

Save electricity:

* Turn off lights when not needed.
* Switch off fans and devices when not using them.

## ⭐ Remember

* Reduce means use less.
* Reuse means use again.
* Recycle means make new things from old things.
* Use dustbins to keep places clean.
* Plant trees to help nature.
* Save water and electricity every day.
* A clean environment keeps Earth healthy.
