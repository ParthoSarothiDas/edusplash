# 🌍 Earth and Space

Earth is our home. We live on Earth with people, animals, and plants.

## 🌎 Our Earth

Earth is the planet where we live.

- 🌍 Earth is our home.
- 🌊 Earth has **land and water**.
- 💧 Most of Earth is covered with water.
- 🌳 Plants, animals, and people live on Earth.
- 🌐 A **globe** is a model of Earth.
- ⚽ Earth is shaped like a ball.

---

## ☀️ The Sun

The Sun is a bright star.

- ☀️ The Sun gives us **light**.
- 🔥 The Sun gives us **heat**.
- 🌱 Plants need sunlight to grow.
- 🌞 We usually see the Sun during the day.

---

## 🌙 The Moon

The Moon is Earth's natural satellite.

- 🌙 We usually see the Moon at night.
- 💡 The Moon does **not** make its own light.
- 🌔 The Moon looks different on different nights.
- 🌕 Sometimes the Moon looks round.
- 🌙 Sometimes it looks like a thin crescent.

---

## ⭐ Stars

Stars are bright objects in the sky.

- ⭐ Stars twinkle at night.
- 🌌 We can see many stars on a clear night.
- ☀️ The Sun is also a star.

---

### Man-made Things

## 🌞 Day and 🌙 Night

### 🌞 Day
- The Sun shines.
- The sky is bright.
- We can play outside.

### 🌙 Night
- The Moon and stars may be seen.
- The sky is dark.
- We sleep at night.
