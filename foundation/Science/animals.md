# 🐾 Animals

Animals are living things. They need **food, water, air, and a home** to live.

Some common animals are:

* 🐶 Dog
* 🐱 Cat
* 🐄 Cow
* 🐐 Goat
* 🐎 Horse
* 🐇 Rabbit
* 🐦 Bird
* 🐟 Fish

---

# 🏡 Animal Homes

| Animal    | Home   |
| --------- | ------ |
| 🐦 Bird   | Nest   |
| 🦁 Lion   | Den    |
| 🐇 Rabbit | Burrow |
| 🐶 Dog    | Kennel |
| 🐎 Horse  | Stable |
| 🐝 Bee    | Hive   |
| 🐟 Fish   | Water  |

---

# 🦴 Animal Body Parts

* 🐦 Birds have **wings** and **feathers**.
* 🐟 Fish have **fins**.
* 🐘 Elephants have a **long trunk**.
* 🐢 Turtles have **hard shells**.
* 🦆 Ducks have **webbed feet**.
* 🦒 Giraffes have **long necks**.

---

# 🏃 How Animals Move

* 🐦 Birds fly.
* 🐟 Fish swim.
* 🐇 Rabbits hop.
* 🐍 Snakes slither.
* 🐎 Horses run.
* 🐒 Monkeys climb trees.

---

# 🍽️ What Animals Eat

* 🐄 Cows eat grass.
* 🐐 Goats eat grass.
* 🦁 Lions eat meat.
* 🐯 Tigers eat meat.
* 🐒 Monkeys like bananas.
* 🐇 Rabbits like carrots.

---

# 🎁 What Animals Give Us

Animals give us many useful things:

* 🥛 **Cow → Milk**
* 🥚 **Hen → Eggs**
* 🧶 **Sheep → Wool**
* 🍯 **Bee → Honey**
