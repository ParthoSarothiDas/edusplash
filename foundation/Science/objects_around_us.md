# 📦 Objects Around Us & Materials

Everything around us is an **object**. We see and use many objects every day at home, at school, and outside.

Some objects are **hard**. Some things can be **poured**. Some things are filled with **air**.

---

# 📦 Objects We Can Hold

We can hold many objects in our hands.

Examples:

- 📖 Book  
- ✏️ Pencil  
- ⚽ Ball  
- 🪑 Chair  
- 🪨 Rock  
- 🍎 Apple  

These objects keep their shape when we hold them.

---

# 💧 Things We Can Pour

Some things can be poured from one container to another.

Examples:

- 💧 Water  
- 🥛 Milk  
- 🧃 Juice  
- 🛢️ Oil  

We can pour them into a **cup**, **glass**, or **bottle**.

---

# 🌬️ Air Around Us

Air is all around us.

- We cannot usually see air.  
- We can feel air when the wind blows.  
- We breathe air every day.  
- 🎈 Balloons are filled with air.  

---

# 🌳 Materials Around Us

Many objects are made from different materials.

| Material | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| 🌳 Wood | Table, Chair, Door, Pencil |
| 🧴 Plastic | Bottle, Bucket, Toy, Comb |
| 🥄 Metal | Spoon, Key, Coin, Nail |
| 🪟 Glass | Window, Bottle, Jar |
| 👕 Fabric | Shirt, Dress, Towel |
| 📄 Paper | Book, Notebook, Newspaper |
| 🛞 Rubber | Eraser, Tyre, Balloon |

---

# ✨ Materials and Their Uses

### 🌳 Wood
- Comes from trees.  
- Wood is strong.  
- Used to make tables, chairs, doors, and pencils.  

### 🧴 Plastic
- Plastic is light.  
- It is used to make bottles, buckets, toys, and combs.  

### 🥄 Metal
- Metal is strong.  
- It is used to make spoons, keys, coins, and nails.  

### 🪟 Glass
- Clear glass lets us see through it.  
- It is used to make windows, bottles, and jars.  
- Glass can break if it falls, so we should be careful.  

### 👕 Fabric
- Fabric is soft.  
- It is used to make shirts, dresses, towels, and other clothes.  

### 📄 Paper
- Paper is used to make books, notebooks, newspapers, and drawings.  

### 🛞 Rubber
- Rubber bends easily.  
- It is used to make erasers, tyres, and balloons.
