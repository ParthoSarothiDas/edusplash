# 🧍 Human Body

Your body is amazing! It helps you see, hear, smell, taste, move, think, and play.

---

# 👀 Body Parts and Their Jobs

| Body Part | What It Does |
|-----------|--------------|
| 👀 Eyes | See |
| 👂 Ears | Hear |
| 👃 Nose | Smell |
| 👅 Tongue | Taste |
| ✋ Hands | Hold things |
| 🦵 Legs | Walk and run |
| 🦷 Teeth | Bite and chew food |
| 🖐️ Skin | Feel hot, cold, and touch |

## ⭐ Remember

- We have **2 hands**.
- We have **10 fingers**.
- Our head is on top of our neck.

---

# ❤️ Inside Our Body

- 🧠 **Brain** – Helps us think.
- ❤️ **Heart** – Keeps beating all day and helps move blood around your body.
- 🫁 **Lungs** – Help us breathe.
- 🦴 **Bones** – Make our body strong and help us stand.
- 💪 **Muscles** – Help us move.

---

# 🥗 Healthy Food

Choose healthy foods every day.

- 🍎 Eat fruits.
- 🥕 Eat vegetables.
- 💧 Drink plenty of water.
- 💪 Healthy foods help us grow and stay strong.
- 🍔 Too much junk food is unhealthy.
- 🍽️ Eat breakfast every day.

---

# 🏃 Exercise

Exercise keeps our body healthy and strong.

Good exercises:

- 🚶 Walking
- 🏃 Running
- 🚴 Cycling
- ⚽ Playing football
- 🤸 Jumping rope
- 🧘 Stretching

Playing outside is fun and good for your body.

---

# 🧼 Stay Clean

Keeping clean helps keep germs away.

Remember to:

- 🧼 Wash your hands before eating and after playing.
- 🪥 Brush your teeth twice a day.
- 🛁 Take a bath regularly.
- 👕 Wear clean clothes.
- ✂️ Keep your fingernails clean and short.
- 🍎 Wash fruits before eating.
- 🤧 Cover your mouth when coughing.
- 🏡 Keep your home and classroom clean.
- 😴 Sleep well every night.
