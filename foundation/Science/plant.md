# 🌱 Plants

Plants are living things. They need **water, air, and sunlight** to grow. Plants make our world green and beautiful.

## 🌿 Parts of a Plant

![plant](plant_parts.png)

### 🌱 Root

* Grows under the ground.
* Holds the plant in the soil.
* Absorbs water from the soil.

### 🌳 Stem

* Keeps the plant standing upright.
* Carries water from the roots to the leaves.

### 🍃 Leaf

* Usually green.
* Makes food for the plant.

### 🌸 Flower

* Grows into a fruit.

### 🍎 Fruit

* Contains seeds.
* Seeds can grow into new plants.

---

## 🌳 Types of Plants

* **Tree** 🌳 – A tall plant with a hard trunk. *(Mango, Banyan)*
* **Shrub** 🌹 – A small plant with many stems. *(Rose)*
* **Herb** 🌿 – A small plant with a soft green stem. *(Mint)*
* **Climber** 🌱 – Needs support to grow. *(Money Plant, Grape Vine)*
* **Creeper** 🍉 – Spreads on the ground. *(Pumpkin, Watermelon)*

---

## 🌍 Why Are Plants Important?

Plants give us:

* 🍎 Food
* 💨 Oxygen to breathe
* 🌳 Shade
* 🪵 Wood
* 👕 Cotton for clothes
* 💊 Medicines

Plants also:

* 🌿 Help keep the air clean.
* 🐦 Give birds and animals a place to live.
