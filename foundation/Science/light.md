# 💡 Light

Light helps us see the world around us. Without light, it is difficult to see.

## ☀️ Sources of Light

Some things give us light.

### 🌞 Natural Light
Natural light comes from nature.

- ☀️ The **Sun** is the biggest natural source of light.
- The Sun gives us light during the day.

### 💡 Man-made Light
People make these light sources.

- 💡 Light Bulb
- 🔦 Torch
- 🕯️ Candle (when it is burning)

> 🌙 **The Moon does not make its own light.** It reflects the Sun's light.

---

## 🌞 Day and 🌙 Night

### 🌞 Day
- The Sun shines.
- It is bright.
- We can see clearly.

### 🌙 Night
- The Sun is not in the sky.
- It is dark.
- We use lights like a torch or a light bulb to see.

---

## 👀 Why Do We Need Light?

Light helps us:
- 👀 See people and objects
- 📚 Read books
- 🎨 Draw and write
- 🚶 Walk safely

Without light, it is hard to see.

---

## 🪟 Things We Can See Through

Some things let light pass through them.

Examples:
- 🪟 Clean glass window
- 🫗 Clear glass cup
- 🐠 Fish tank
- 💧 Clean water

These help us see what is behind them.

Some things do **not** let light pass through.

Examples:
- 🧱 Brick wall
- 🚪 Wooden door
- 📖 Book

---

## 🌑 Shadows

A **shadow** is a dark shape.

A shadow is made when **light is blocked** by an object.

Things that can make shadows:
- 👦 Person
- 🌳 Tree
- ⚽ Ball
- 📚 Book
- 🚗 Toy

You can often see your shadow on a sunny day.

---

## 🏡 Light Around Us

We use light every day.

- 💡 Light bulb lights our room.
- 🔦 Torch helps us see in the dark.
- 🕯️ Candle gives light during a power cut.
- ☀️ The Sun gives us light and warmth.

---

# ⭐ Remember

- ☀️ The Sun is a natural source of light.
- 💡 Light bulbs, torches, and candles give us light.
- 👀 We need light to see.
- 🌙 The Moon does not make its own light.
- 🪟 Clean glass lets us see through it.
- 🌑 A shadow is made when light is blocked.
- 🌳 Trees, people, and toys can make shadows.
- 🔦 A torch helps us see in the dark.
